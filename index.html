The Couple

Today, they live in Germany. A long way from both countries, and exactly where the two of them found each other.

Our Story

From two countries to one city, and to each other

I

Two roads

Maheshika set out from Colombo and Moksha from Tirupati, and both roads ended in the same city in Chemnitz, Germany. Different master's degrees, different lecture halls, different circles of friends. On paper there was no reason for their paths to cross.

II

A friendship

Destiny had other ideas, and it chose the most ordinary place of all: a part-time job. Shifts turned into conversations, conversations into a friendship, and the friendship into the steadiest thing in a life far from home. In a country where neither of them had family, the friends each of them made became family to them both, and these two became each other's.

III

A new beginning

Somewhere along the way, without any single dramatic moment, the friendship grew into something more. Two strangers in a foreign city are now two families in two countries, celebrating with two weddings: one in Tirupati and one on a poruwa in Kalutara. This is the first page of the next chapter, and we are so glad you are reading it with us.

Traditions

A short guide to a Sri Lankan poruwa wedding

For friends joining us from India, Germany and further afield, here is what happens on the poruwa, in a few lines each.

  1. 01

    Magul bera

    Traditional drummers announce the start. The couple walk in to the beat of the magul bera, with dancers leading the way.

  2. 02

    The poruwa

    A decorated wooden platform, roofed with flowers and gokkola. The bride and groom step up onto it, each with the right foot first.

  3. 03

    Betel leaves

    The couple hand each other sheaves of betel leaves and let them fall on the poruwa: a greeting to the elders and to those who came before.

  4. 04

    Tying the little fingers

    The bride's maternal uncle ties the couple's little fingers together with a gold thread and pours water over them. Like the water, what is joined here cannot be taken back.

  5. 05

    The gifts

    The groom places a gold necklace on the bride, and gives a length of white cloth to her mother, a thank-you for raising his bride.

  6. 06

    Kiribath

    The couple feed each other milk rice, the dish every Sri Lankan beginning is marked with.

  7. 07

    Jayamangala Gatha

    Young girls in white sing the verses of blessing while the couple stand on the poruwa.

  8. 08

    The lamp and the coconut

    The couple light the brass oil lamp together and step down from the poruwa. As they do, a coconut is split, to keep bad luck away from the marriage.

Travel & Stay

Getting to Kalutara

Anantara Kalutara Resort sits where the Kalu Ganga river meets the Indian Ocean, about 42 km south of Colombo.

Getting there

  • By road. Under an hour from Colombo on the Southern Expressway (exit at Dodangoda), or the slower coastal Galle Road. From the airport allow 1.5 to 2 hours. Parking is available at the resort.
  • Taxis. PickMe and Uber run throughout the area, and the resort can arrange a transfer if you ask when booking.
  • By train. The coastal line from Colombo stops at Kalutara South, a short tuk-tuk ride from the resort.
