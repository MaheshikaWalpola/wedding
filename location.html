Where We Say I Do

Anantara Kalutara Resort

St. Sebastian's Road, Katukurunda, Kalutara, Sri Lanka

Getting There

The resort sits where the Kalu Ganga river meets the Indian Ocean, about 42 km south of Colombo. From Bandaranaike International Airport it's roughly a 1.5–2 hour drive via the Southern Expressway (exit at Dodangoda) or the scenic Galle Road along the coast.

Taxis and ride-hailing apps (PickMe, Uber) operate across the region, and the resort can arrange airport transfers on request. If you're coming by train, Kalutara South station is a short tuk-tuk ride away.

Discover Sri Lanka

Before You Fly

Visa (ETA)

Most nationalities need an online Electronic Travel Authorisation — apply at eta.gov.lk a couple of weeks before flying. It's quick and inexpensive.

Money

The currency is the Sri Lankan Rupee (LKR). Cards work in hotels, but keep cash for tuk-tuks and markets — airport and bank ATMs are the easiest way to withdraw.

SIM & WhatsApp

Grab a cheap tourist SIM/eSIM (Dialog or Mobitel) at the airport — generous data for a few euros. Sri Lanka runs on WhatsApp, just like home. UK-style three-pin plugs (Type G) — bring an adapter.

Once You Land

Getting Around

PickMe & Taxis

PickMe is the local Uber (Uber works too) — cheap, cashless, everywhere. For longer trips, hotels arrange drivers at fair fixed rates.

Tuk-tuks

The joyful way to travel short hops. Agree the price first or ask for the meter — and hold on to your hat. 🛺

Trains

The coastal line from Colombo to Kalutara South hugs the ocean — slow, scenic and wonderfully cheap. The hill-country train to Ella is one of the world's great rides if you stay longer.

Make a Holiday of It

Around Kalutara

Madu River Safari

A boat ride through mangrove tunnels and island temples — cinnamon, monkeys and kingfishers. ~30 minutes from the venue; perfect for the morning after.

Bentota & Turtles

Golden beaches, water sports, and the sea-turtle hatchery at Kosgoda where you can meet day-old turtles. ~25 minutes south.

Galle Fort

A 17th-century Dutch fort town of ramparts, cafés and boutiques — the prettiest sunset walls in the country. ~1 hour south.

Tea Country

Staying a week? The misty hills of Ella and Nuwara Eliya — waterfalls, tea factories and that famous train — are an unforgettable add-on.

Taste the Island

Eat & Drink

Rice & curry is a universe, not a dish — a dozen little bowls of wonder. Hunt down hoppers (crispy bowl-shaped pancakes, best with an egg), kottu roti (the midnight street-food drumbeat of Sri Lanka), fresh king coconut straight from the shell, and real Ceylon tea. December is 27–30°C — light clothes, sunscreen, and an appetite are all you need. 🥥
