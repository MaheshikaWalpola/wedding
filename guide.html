Welcome to Sri Lanka

A guest guide to the island

The Madu River boat safari through the mangroves is half an hour away. Bentota's beaches and the Kosgoda turtle hatchery are 25 minutes south, and Galle Fort, with its ramparts and cafés, about an hour. December is warm, around 27 to 30°C, with the odd short shower. Light clothes, sunscreen and an appetite for rice and curry are all you need.

A suggested week

One way to see the island around the wedding

This is the route we would take with our own guests. Pick the days that suit you; nothing here is compulsory, and the wedding is the only fixed point.

  1. Day 1

    Arrival and Colombo

    Land at Katunayake and, depending on the hour, ease into Colombo: Port City, Gangaramaya Temple, Galle Face Green, Independence Memorial Hall, the Lotus Tower (about USD 20 a person for the observation deck and the digital art museum, 1 to 1.5 hours), Seema Malaka on the lake, and Pettah Market if there is time.

  2. Day 2

    The wedding

    Tea from 4 PM, the poruwa at 5:25 PM, then the hall until late. For the ladies, hair and dressing can be arranged at a salon in Piliyandala; tell us and we will book it.

  3. Day 3

    A slow morning, then Colombo

    Sleep in. Whatever Colombo missed on the first day, and some shopping: Ceylon tea, spices, batik and linen, Ayurveda products.

  4. Day 4

    Kandy, on the way to the hills

    The Temple of the Sacred Tooth Relic (light-coloured clothing, shoulders and knees covered), Kandy Lake and the Kandy View Point. If there is time and energy: the Royal Botanical Gardens at Peradeniya. The Ambuluwawa Tower is a detour of about an hour each way and a narrow spiral climb, better for a small group.

  5. Day 5

    Nuwara Eliya

    Gregory Lake for a short stop, a scenic drive through Horton Plains, a factory tour and tasting at Pedro Tea Estate (about LKR 500 to 1,000 a person), and the waterfalls at Lover's Leap and Bambarakanda. Pack a warm layer: the hills drop to 10 to 15 degrees at night.

  6. Day 6

    The train to Ella

    The morning train from Nanu Oya to Ella is one of the great rides; take it in this direction. In Ella: the Nine Arch Bridge, Ravana Falls, Diyaluma Falls, and Lipton's Seat if time permits. Little Adam's Peak is a gentle hike for the keen.

  7. Day 7

    Yala and Kataragama

    A dawn safari in Yala National Park (5:30 AM pick-up; leopards, elephants, and a jeep we can recommend), then the Kataragama Temple.

  8. Day 8

    Galle and the south coast

    Galle Fort with its lighthouse and clock tower, Jungle Beach, the Japanese Peace Pagoda, and the beaches at Unawatuna, Mirissa and Ahangama.

  9. Last evening

    Dinner at home

    Before you fly, dinner with the family at Maheshika's home in Piliyandala. The address comes with your invitation, not on this page.

Things to do

By area

Around the wedding

Kalutara beach and sunset, a Kalu Ganga boat ride, the Kalutara Bodhiya temple, Richmond Castle, Brief Garden by Bevis Bawa, Bentota beach and water sports, the Madu River boat safari, the Kosgoda turtle hatchery.

Easiest after the wedding: a Bentota or Madu River boat safari. Close and gentle.

South coast

Whale and dolphin watching from Mirissa (November to April), Galle Fort's ramparts, lighthouse and boutiques, snorkelling and turtles at Hikkaduwa, surfing at Weligama (good for beginners), Coconut Tree Hill and the beach bars of Mirissa.

Hill country

Tea estate and factory tours near Nuwara Eliya, the Nuwara Eliya to Ella train (ride it in that direction), Ella's Nine Arch Bridge, Little Adam's Peak, Ella Rock and Ravana Falls, and Horton Plains with World's End at dawn.

Cultural triangle

Sigiriya, the Dambulla cave temple, Polonnaruwa and Anuradhapura. We do not recommend the Pinnawala elephant orphanage; the animals are not well treated there.

Kandy

The Temple of the Sacred Tooth, a Kandyan dance show, Kandy Lake and the Peradeniya botanical gardens.

Safari and wildlife

Udawalawe for elephants (easy, reliable, good with families) or Yala for leopards; go at dawn. Minneriya for the elephant gathering, paired with Sigiriya. Wilpattu is quiet with fewer jeeps; Kumana is remote, for longer trips.

If you only see a handful of things

  • Sigiriya Rock Fortress: the Lion Rock, frescoes and huge views. Climb at dawn.
  • Galle Fort: a Dutch colonial fort by the sea, ramparts and boutiques.
  • Temple of the Sacred Tooth: the island's holiest Buddhist shrine, in Kandy. Wear light colours.
  • Nine Arch Bridge: the railway bridge in the Ella hills.
  • Yala National Park: the best place in Sri Lanka to see leopards.
  • Dambulla Cave Temple: ancient cave murals and rows of Buddha statues.
  • Nuwara Eliya: emerald tea plantations, cool air and colonial charm.
  • Mirissa: blue whales and dolphins by boat, November to April.
  • Anuradhapura and Polonnaruwa: vast ancient royal cities, best by bicycle.
  • Adam's Peak: a dawn pilgrimage climb, December to May.

Good to know

Getting around and local tips

Transport

PickMe is the local ride app, like Uber: cheapest and reliable, get it before you arrive. Uber works in Colombo and on the west coast. Tuk-tuks are everywhere: book through PickMe for a fixed fare, or agree the price first. A car and driver is the easiest way to tour; arrange it through your hotel. Trains are scenic but slow. Skip self-driving.

Money

The currency is the Rupee (LKR). Carry cash: many tuk-tuks, shops and temples are cash only. ATMs are in towns; cards work at hotels. Tip a little at restaurants when there is no service charge, round up tuk-tuks, and tip drivers and guides.

Staying connected

Buy a tourist SIM or eSIM at the airport (Dialog or Mobitel). Everyone uses WhatsApp. Plugs are the three-pin Type G and Type D, 230 V, so bring an adapter.

Temples and etiquette

Cover shoulders and knees, remove shoes and hats, carry a scarf. Never turn your back to a Buddha statue for a photo.

Be a little careful

Some drivers steer you to gem or spice shops on commission; decline. Agree every price upfront. Swim only where there are flags or lifeguards; currents can be strong. Monkeys grab food, phones and sunglasses at temples and Sigiriya. Drink bottled water, use repellent, wear SPF 50 or higher, and watch belongings in crowds.

Weather and packing

December is dry and sunny on the west and south coast, around 27 to 30 degrees. The hill country is cold at night, about 10 to 15 degrees, so pack a warm layer. Bring light clothes, swimwear, sunscreen, repellent and the plug adapter.

Food and shopping

Try rice and curry, hoppers and string hoppers, kottu roti, fresh seafood, curd and treacle, king coconut and the short eats. Bring home Ceylon tea, cinnamon and spices, batik and linen, Ayurveda products, and sapphires only from trusted shops.

Before you fly

The visa, and numbers to save

Electronic Travel Authorisation (ETA)

German and Indian passport holders need an ETA before flying: a free online pre-approval linked to your passport. Without it airlines can refuse boarding. Apply only at the official site, eta.gov.lk (look-alike sites charge fake fees): choose Apply as Individual, then Tourist ETA. Approval comes by email in one to three days; apply two to three weeks ahead and save or print it. Your passport must be valid for six months or more.
