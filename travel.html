Travel guide

Coming to Sri Lanka for the wedding

Everything you need before you fly and once you land: flights, the visa, money, a SIM card, getting around, and a few ideas if you can stay a little longer. Hotels near the venue are listed on the main page.

Flying in

  • Bandaranaike International Airport (CMB), north of Colombo, is where you will land. The resort is about 1.5 to 2 hours away by road, on the Southern Expressway (exit at Dodangoda) or along the coast on Galle Road.
  • Taxis and transfers. PickMe and Uber both run from the airport, and the resort can arrange a transfer if you ask when booking.
  • By train. The coastal line from Colombo hugs the ocean. Kalutara South station is a short tuk-tuk ride from the resort.

The venue sits where the Kalu Ganga river meets the Indian Ocean, about 42 km south of Colombo.

Where to stay

Anantara Kalutara Resort is the venue, and the most sensible place to sleep: you wake up steps from the poruwa. Avani Kalutara is next door, with Royal Palms and Turyaa a few minutes along the coast.

Book early and mention the wedding. The full list, with map links, is under Travel & Stay on the main page.

Before you fly

Visa

Most nationalities need an online Electronic Travel Authorisation. Apply at eta.gov.lk a couple of weeks before flying. It is quick and inexpensive.

Money

The currency is the Sri Lankan Rupee. Cards work in hotels, but keep cash for tuk-tuks and markets. Airport and bank ATMs are the easiest way to withdraw.

SIM & plugs

A tourist SIM or eSIM from Dialog or Mobitel at the airport gives you generous data for a few euros. Sri Lanka runs on WhatsApp. Plugs are Type G (UK style) and Type D, so bring an adapter.

Getting around

PickMe & taxis

PickMe is the local ride-hailing app, and Uber works too: cheap, cashless, everywhere. For longer trips, hotels arrange drivers at fair fixed rates.

Tuk-tuks

The joyful way to travel short hops. Agree the price first or ask for the meter, and hold on to your hat.

Trains

The coastal line is slow, scenic and wonderfully cheap. If you stay longer, the hill-country train to Ella is one of the great rides.

If you have a few days to spare

The Madu River boat safari through the mangroves is half an hour away. Bentota's beaches and the Kosgoda turtle hatchery are 25 minutes south, and Galle Fort, with its ramparts and cafés, about an hour. December is warm, around 27 to 30°C, with the odd short shower. Light clothes, sunscreen and an appetite for rice and curry are all you need.
