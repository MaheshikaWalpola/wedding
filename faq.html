Good to Know

Questions & Answers

Everything guests usually ask — and if your question isn't here, just reach out to us.

When should I RSVP by?

Please reply by 21 October 2026 through the RSVP page — it takes less than a minute, and it helps us plan your seat and your plate.

What should I wear?

For the poruwa ceremony: Sri Lankan elegant — sarees, osariya, or smart traditional wear are warmly encouraged. For the evening: cocktail attire, jewel tones welcome. Please leave white and cream for the couple. Parts of the celebration are outdoors on garden lawns, so choose comfortable shoes.

Are children welcome?

Absolutely — little ones are part of the family! Please include them in your guest count when you RSVP so we can seat everyone comfortably.

What about gifts?

Your love and blessings are the greatest gift. Should you wish to give more, a small envelope is warmly treasured — there will be a place for them at the reception. Please, no boxed gifts — suitcases home are heavy enough!

What's the weather like in December?

Kalutara in December is warm and tropical — around 27–30°C in the day, pleasantly balmy in the evening. A brief shower is always possible, but the celebration is prepared for it. Light fabrics are your friend.

How do I get there, and where do I park?

The resort is about 1.5–2 hours from Colombo airport via the Southern Expressway (exit Dodangoda). Parking is available at the resort, and taxis and PickMe/Uber operate throughout the area. More details on the Location page.

Can I share the photos I take?

Yes, please! Upload your favourite moments to our Photo Wall — they appear right on this website for everyone to enjoy, and they become part of our memory of the day.

How do I find my seat at the reception?

Type your name on the Find Your Seat page — your table number will appear closer to the big day.
