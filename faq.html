Good to know

Questions and answers

What guests usually ask. If your question is not here, we are a message away.

When should I reply by?

Please reply by 30th October 2026 through the RSVP form. It takes less than a minute, and it helps us plan your seat.

What should I wear?

Sri Lankan elegant. A saree, an osariya (Kandyan), a dress or a suit, or simply whatever you feel lovely in. Please note that the poruwa ceremony is held outdoors for about an hour, while the rest of the evening is inside the air-conditioned hall.

Are children welcome?

Absolutely. Little ones are part of the family. Please count them in when you reply, so we can seat everyone comfortably. At dinner, small children can share a plate with you: the hotel counts the dinner plates, not the guests, so a shared plate keeps the count right (more on that in the guest guide).

What about gifts?

While we appreciate your love and blessings above all, we kindly request no boxed gifts. Should you wish to give something, a small envelope is warmly treasured, and there will be envelopes and a pen at the reception. From abroad, PayPal (walpolamaheshika@gmail.com) works just as well.

What is the weather like in December?

Kalutara in December is warm and tropical, around 27 to 30°C in the day and balmy in the evening. A brief shower is always possible, and the celebration is prepared for it. Light fabrics are your friend.

How do I get there, and where do I park?

The resort is about 1.5 to 2 hours from the airport on the Southern Expressway (exit Dodangoda). Parking is available at the resort, and taxis, PickMe and Uber run throughout the area. More in Travel, and hotels near the venue are in the guest guide.

Is there a guide to visiting Sri Lanka?

Yes: the guest guide has things to do near the venue and across the island, where to stay from five star to boutique, getting around, the visa and emergency numbers.

Can I share the photos I take?

Yes, please. Add them on the photos page. They go straight into our album, where everyone can see them and save them.

How do I find my seat at the reception?

Type your name in Find your seat. Your table number appears there closer to the day.
