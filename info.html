Monday, 21 December 2026 · From 5 PM

The Day's Celebrations

A traditional poruwa ceremony followed by an evening reception — here's how the day unfolds.

5:00 PM

Guest Arrival & Welcome Drinks

Arrive at the resort's garden pavilion and enjoy a refreshing welcome drink as the magul bera drummers begin.

5:30 PM

Poruwa Ceremony

The oil lamp is lit, the ashtaka blessed — the traditional ceremony on the poruwa, with the tying of the pirith noola and the breaking of the coconut.

6:30 PM

Blessings & Register

Rings, signatures and the first married photographs as the light turns golden.

7:00 PM

Sunset Cocktails

Cocktails by the lagoon as the sun goes down over the water — while the couple steals away for photographs.

8:00 PM

Reception & Dinner

The newlyweds' grand entrance, dinner and toasts under the palms.

9:30 PM

Cake & First Dance

Something sweet, then the floor is theirs — and right after, it's yours.

10:00 PM

Dancing Until Late

Baila, hits and bare feet on the lawn — dance until the stars give up before you do.

What to Wear

Dress Code

Poruwa Ceremony

Sri Lankan elegant — sarees, osariya, or smart traditional wear are warmly encouraged. Light colours suit the afternoon sun; please leave white and cream for the couple.

Reception

Evening cocktail attire — jewel tones welcome. The reception is partly outdoors by the lagoon, so choose comfortable shoes for garden lawns and dancing.

Questions about the day? Reach out to the wedding party — details will be shared closer to the date.
